Three leaks. One root cause: nobody's watching.

Service-based small businesses — contractors, agencies, salons, med/dental, consultants, repair shops — lose revenue to three operational leaks. Each leak is automatable, and each has been packaged into a named, guaranteed offer.

Scheduling, Dispatch & No-Shows

Phone-tag, double-booking, no reminders, high-friction rescheduling.

  • Calls go unanswered and the lead phones a competitor next.
  • Bookings are made by hand, so double-bookings and travel-time clashes happen.
  • No reminder sequence means empty slots on tomorrow's calendar.
  • Rescheduling takes a phone call, so people simply don't show.

Quoting & Estimate Generation

Slow manual quotes, pricing trapped in one person's head, no follow-up after the quote is sent.

  • Quotes take days because every one is built from scratch.
  • Pricing logic lives in the owner's head, so nobody else can quote.
  • Sent quotes are never followed up on.
  • The business that quotes first usually wins the job — even if it isn't the cheapest.

Post-Service Follow-Up

No owner of the review/rebooking/referral step, no systematic timing, awkward in-person asks.

  • Nobody owns the follow-up step once a job is marked done.
  • Timing is manual and inconsistent — asks happen sporadically, not systematically.
  • No visibility into rebooking windows for recurring-service customers.
  • Asking for referrals in person feels awkward, so it rarely happens.
  • Review requests go out only when someone remembers, so most happy customers are never asked.

Each leak is measurable. That's exactly what our diagnostic finds — in your business, with your numbers.
