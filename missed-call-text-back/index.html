Missed call text-back and AI call answering for service businesses

When you're under a sink, up a ladder or with a client, the phone rings out. The caller doesn't leave a voicemail. They phone the next business on the list.

The Never-Miss System makes sure that call still turns into a booking.

Calculate my lost revenue — free → WhatsApp us

What it does

  • Answers calls and chats with AI, 24/7, and books the job straight into your calendar, even after hours.
  • Texts back every missed call within moments, so the caller hears from you before they dial a competitor.
  • Books around your day, with buffer and travel-time rules so you don't double-book or get squeezed between jobs.
  • Sends a three-step reminder sequence at 48 hours, 24 hours and 2 hours before the appointment, so empty slots stop appearing on tomorrow's calendar.
  • Lets customers reschedule in one click, instead of phoning you to do it.

Your own numbers first

Before you commit, we pull a missed-call baseline from your own call log. You'll see what the problem is costing you in rands, and that number is yours to keep whether or not you work with us. Want a quick estimate now? Try the missed call cost calculator.

The guarantee

We'll recover the revenue you're losing in missed bookings in your first 14 days, measured against your own missed-call log, or we run it free until we do. You keep everything we built.

Who it's for

Plumbers, electricians, HVAC and repair businesses, salons, medical and dental practices, and consultants: any business where the phone is the front door and the person who answers it is also the person doing the work.
