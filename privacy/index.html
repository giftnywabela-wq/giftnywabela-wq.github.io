Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal information we collect on cogwell.co.za, why, and what your rights are.

Who we are

Cogwell is an AI automation business serving small service businesses in South Africa. Cogwell is the responsible party for personal information collected through cogwell.co.za, as defined in the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Contact us at giftnywabela@gmail.com or 062 110 9631.

What we collect

When you send us an enquiry or request a diagnostic, we collect the details you type into the form: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number (optional), business name, what you are interested in, and any message you write.

If you contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email, we also keep that conversation.

If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics records how the site is used: pages viewed, clicks on call, WhatsApp and email links, device and browser type, and approximate location.

Why we use it

We use your details to reply to you, to deliver a diagnostic or service you ask for, and to follow up on your enquiry. We use analytics to understand which pages work and which do not. We do not sell your personal information.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Form submissions are stored in our cloud database (hosted by Lovable Cloud, which is built on Supabase) and copied to a Google Sheet that we use to manage enquiries. Email and WhatsApp conversations sit in our Google and WhatsApp accounts. These providers process data on our behalf and may store it outside South Africa. We only use providers that apply appropriate security safeguards.

Cookies and analytics

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. If you click Accept, Google Analytics sets cookies to measure site use. If you click Decline, no analytics cookies are set. Google Analytics may still receive basic signals that cannot be tied to you, such as the fact that a page was viewed.

We do not use advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only for as long as we need them to respond, to provide our services and to meet legal or accounting requirements. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your rights

Under POPIA you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, and withdraw consent you have given. Email or call us using the details above and we will respond.

If you are not happy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice. The latest version is always on this page. Last updated: 18 October 2026.
